Using the beta

Your content

  • Your footage, music and projects stay yours.
  • Only use media you have the right to use, including music and other people's likeness. Don't use Edit Bay to make content that's illegal, or that harasses, deceives or infringes on others.

Claude features

  • Claude's suggestions and edits are made by AI and can be wrong. Review them before you publish.
  • When you use these features, what's sent to Anthropic is covered in the Privacy page, and Anthropic's own terms apply.
  • Every Claude edit saves a version first, so you can undo it.
