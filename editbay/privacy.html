Privacy

Edit Bay Beta 2 · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: your footage and projects stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded while you edit, unless you use Claude's features or you're signed in on claude.ai, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Media: video, audio and images you import are stored in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on this device.
  • Projects: timelines, versions, settings and transcripts are stored the same way.
  • Transcription: in-browser captions run on your device. The speech model comes from Edit Bay itself or, on the hosted site, from Hugging Face. Your audio is never sent anywhere. If an AI is set up, the caption text (not the audio) is sent to it to correct names and punctuation.
  • Generated music and sound effects are made on your device. The AI, if set up, receives the edit's timings and title text to plan the music.
  • Exports: exported videos, still frames and project files are made on your device and saved where you choose.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of this. Use File › Save Project File to keep a backup.

What leaves your device, and when

  • AI features (Create, the AI chat, Review, Smart Auto Cut) use the AI you choose in Help › AI Settings:
    • On this device: an open model runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The model downloads once from Hugging Face.
    • Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq or Claude: a text description of your timeline (clip names, timings, titles, captions, markers and transcript text) is sent to that company's API, using the key you entered. If the model can see images, up to eight small preview frames go too. Media files themselves are never sent. That company handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy. Free tiers may use what you send to improve their models, so check their terms.
    • Ollama or LM Studio: requests go to your own computer.
    • On claude.ai: the same description and preview frames go to Anthropic's Claude through your claude.ai account.
    API keys are stored only in your browser and sent only to the service they belong to.
  • On claude.ai: if you open Edit Bay as a claude.ai Artifact while signed in, your projects and media files up to 20 MB sync to private storage tied to your account. Nobody else can read them, including the app's owner.
  • Feedback: what you write in the Feedback form, plus optional technical details (browser, screen size, timeline length) if you tick that box. On claude.ai it goes to the app's owner. On the hosted site, it opens a public GitHub issue that you review and post yourself.
  • Crash reports: on claude.ai, error messages are sent to the owner automatically, with no media or project content.
  • Usage counts (off unless you turn them on): how many times each tool was used, and whether exports succeeded. No media, file names, titles or text you type. They're sent with your feedback. Turn them off in Help › Usage Counts.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when fonts load.
  • Captions and the on-device AI: the models and their library come from Hugging Face and jsDelivr, but only when you use those features.
  • GitHub hosts the public test site and receives standard web request logs.

Edit Bay sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Most data never leaves your device, and you control it there. To ask about or delete anything sent to the owner (feedback, crash reports or usage counts), open an issue at github.com/Joseph-ops-java/OTR/issues. If you're in the EU or UK, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission).

Changes

This is a beta. If what Edit Bay collects changes, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
