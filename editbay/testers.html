Thanks for testing Edit Bay

Beta 2 · a video editor that runs in your browser

Edit Bay is free and needs no account. Your footage and projects stay in your browser. This is a beta, so things will break: when they do, telling us is the most useful thing you can do.

1. Get set up (2 minutes)

  • Best on: Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. Safari, Firefox, tablets and phones work with limits, and testing them helps too.
  • Install it (optional): in Chrome or Edge, click Install app at the top. Edit Bay then gets its own window and icon, and opens offline.
  • Check your browser: Help › Check This Browser shows what will and won't work on your computer.
  • AI features (optional): Help › AI Settings. The easiest free option is a Google Gemini key from aistudio.google.com (no card needed). Or use “On this device” for simple requests, with no account at all.

2. Try these (about 15 minutes)

  1. Open the sample. On the welcome screen, choose Try with sample media. Play it.
  2. Make a quick edit. Drag a clip to reorder it, trim one by dragging its edge, then press T to add a title.
  3. Bring your own footage. Start a new project and import 2 or 3 clips from your phone or camera, including at least one video shot on a phone.
  4. Captions. If a clip has speech, choose Auto captions in the Media panel.
  5. Colour. Open the Color page (⌘2 or Ctrl+2) and try a look.
  6. Ask the AI (if you set it up): in the AI tab, try “add a title that says … at the start” or “make it 15 seconds”.
  7. Export. Click Export, choose Social, and play the file it saves.

3. Tell us how it went

  • Click Feedback in the top bar (or Help › Send Feedback). On this site it opens a short GitHub issue, already filled in. You need a free GitHub account to post it.
  • Most useful: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and which browser and computer you used. Tick “Include technical details” and the browser, screen size and timeline length are added for you, never your media.
  • Optional: in Help › Usage Counts you can share anonymous counts of which tools you used. No media, file names or text.

Good to know

  • Your work lives in this browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers loses it. Use File › Save Project File to keep a copy.
  • iPhone video: HEVC (“High Efficiency”) clips only open in browsers that can decode them. If one won't open, Edit Bay tells you why and what to change.
  • Big files are fine. Originals are stored on your device, limited only by free disk space.
