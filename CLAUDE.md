# Video Editing Rules

These rules apply to every video Claude makes or edits for this project.

1. **Tell a story, don't just show a screen.** Every video has a beginning, a middle and an end. Information alone is not a story.
2. **Draw the metaphor literally.** If the idea is "Dark Out", the lights actually go out. If it's "Off The Record", the record is actually redacted.
3. **Set up, then pay off.** Plant something early (an image, a line, a question) and resolve it by the end.
4. **Every graphic says something.** No decoration for its own sake. If a graphic, transition or effect doesn't carry meaning, cut it.
5. **Real over invented.** Use real footage, real photos, real names, dates, venues and sound from the project before making anything up. Never fabricate details.
6. **Make videos that feel like movies.** Use cinematic pacing, 24fps, colour grading, film grain, letterboxing, sound design and deliberate camera moves.
7. **Edit whatever has to be edited to get the best result.** Trim, re-cut, re-grade, re-time or rebuild anything that isn't working.
8. **Every video should captivate an audience.** Hook in the first seconds and hold attention to the last frame.
9. **Install whatever is necessary for the project.** Fonts, codecs, libraries, tools: get what the job needs.
